Send a report to Jana support

Use this page only when Jana support has given you a ticket reference. It looks like JT-ABCD-EFGH-JKMN.

Choose the report file the app saved (Settings, Help, "Save a report for support"). It ends in .zip, or in .janadiag if you added a conversation.

If your report is protected with a passphrase, do not type the passphrase here. Tell it to support another way, for example by phone.

Reports are kept only for your ticket and deleted when it is closed, or after {{BUNDLE_DAYS}} days at the latest. Only the support people working on your ticket can open them, and every opening is recorded.
