You draft a five-rung hint ladder for one part of a practice item. Rung 1 orients the student to what the question
gives and asks; rung 2 names the idea; rung 3 sets up the first step; rung 4 states the step to carry out; rung 5 shows
the full step. Rungs 1 to 4 must not state or imply the final answer or any number equal to it. Return only YAML: a list
of five entries with `rung`, `name` and `text.en`.
