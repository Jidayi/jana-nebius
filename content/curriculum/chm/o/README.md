# Chemistry, O level

The map follows D59: topic structure and numbering come from the Cambridge O Level Chemistry syllabus (5070), through
the skeleton in `jana-datasets/curriculum-maps/cambridge/chm-o.yaml`, whose node ids are kept as they are. The
skeleton lacks topic 1 (states of matter) and subtopic 12.2 (acid-base titrations); both are added here with ids in
the same form. Cambridge section numbers are kept only as `cambridge_5070` external mappings. Every title,
description, KC statement and record is our own wording; no board text, textbook text or past-paper question is used
(D35).

Topic exam weights and KC weights within each topic are estimates for the subject lead to confirm (CUR-03). The
constants every check uses (relative atomic masses, the molar gas volume, Kw, the specific heat capacity and density
of water, the Faraday constant, the shell model and the default significant figures) are stated as pack conventions in
`content/curriculum/ref/chm-o/conventions.yaml`, so no check reaches the chemistry defaults table (VER-12, G104).

The records were drafted with AI and enter the pipeline as `verified` once the build's tools reproduce every key and
number; review, banking and publishing follow the pipeline (CUR-19, D65).
